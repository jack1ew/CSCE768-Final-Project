================================================================================
COMEBACK ANALYSIS WITH ECONOMIC FACTORS
================================================================================

KEY FINDINGS:

1. ECONOMIC STATE MATTERS FOR COMEBACKS:
   • Teams with full buy capability have better comeback chances
   • Being forced to eco while behind dramatically reduces comeback odds
   • Equipment differential compounds score differential effects

2. COMEBACK THRESHOLDS:
   • Behind 5+ at Round 15 + Eco = ~10-15% comeback rate
   • Behind 5+ at Round 15 + Full Buy = ~20-25% comeback rate
   • Behind 3-4 at Round 15 + Full Buy = ~35-40% comeback rate

3. CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS:
   • Maintaining economic viability while behind is crucial
   • Teams that comeback typically have higher equipment at Round 15
   • Score deficit alone doesn't tell full story - economy matters

================================================================================
IMPLICATIONS:
================================================================================
• Economic management is critical when behind
• Saving for future rounds may be better than forcing low-% plays
• Equipment differential should be considered with score differential
• Full buy capability gives 'breathing room' for comebacks
